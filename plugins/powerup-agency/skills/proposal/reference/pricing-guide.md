# Planning and pricing guide

For **planning** only. Real prices always come from the user or `.powerup/agency.md`.

## Three options that sell
- **Essential:** solves the core problem, nothing extra. Lets a tight budget say yes.
- **Recommended:** what you'd do if it were your business. Most clients pick this one when it's clearly explained.
- **Complete:** adds the extras that compound results (e.g. SEO setup, content, care plan, training).

Make the difference between options about outcomes ("ready for Google from day one"), not just more pages.

## Typical durations (small-business websites)
Ranges vary a lot by team and client speed — use them to sanity-check, then follow the user's numbers.

| Work | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Discovery + sitemap | 1 week |
| Design (homepage + key templates) | 1–3 weeks |
| Build on Webflow / Framer | 1–3 weeks |
| Build on WordPress / Shopify (theme customisation) | 2–4 weeks |
| Content entry / migration | 0.5–2 weeks |
| QA, launch, redirects | 0.5–1 week |
| SEO audit | 2–5 days |

Add client feedback time explicitly (e.g. "3 working days per review round").

## Scope that prevents arguments
- Count things: pages, templates, revision rounds, languages, products imported.
- Name the platform and who pays its fees (Webflow site plan, Shopify plan, paid plugins, fonts, stock photos).
- State who writes the copy and supplies photos.
- Say what happens after launch: support period, care plan, training.

## Never
- Invent a price, discount or deadline.
- Promise rankings or traffic numbers. Promise actions and how success will be measured.
- Copy a competitor's proposal text.
