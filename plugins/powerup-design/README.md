# Power-Up Kit — Design

**Skill:** `brand-sheet` — reads a website's CSS (or a brand guide, or Figma when connected) and builds a brand sheet: colours with likely roles and WCAG contrast checks, heading and body fonts and where they're loaded from, logo candidates, and tone of voice from the site's copy. Renders a printable one-page HTML sheet and saves the results to the client's memory, clearly marking what is confirmed and what is a guess.

Requires Node.js 18+ and installs **powerup-core** automatically.

Docs: https://github.com/junaid-creo/power-up-kit
